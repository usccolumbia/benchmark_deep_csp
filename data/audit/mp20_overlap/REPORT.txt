INTERPRETATION
Zero material-ID intersection does not establish absence of structural overlap. Different MP IDs can contain structures that match the training/validation examples. The earlier project documentation describes ID-based exclusion; that cannot detect these cases.
Standard-fit results are operational matches under the stated benchmark tolerance. They are not a claim of numerically identical coordinates, nor proof that every match is the same experimentally distinguishable polymorph. The supplied report uses the word identical too broadly.
For example, the reported Ag2S pair matches the standard tolerance but fails the tighter no-scaling check. Multiple reported pairs have normalized RMS near machine precision and also pass the tighter test.
The tighter test provides additional evidence of near-duplicate geometry; it is not a universal leakage definition. A benchmark policy should state its matching tolerance and whether volume rescaling is allowed.

METHOD
pymatgen 2026.5.4. Parsed every unique CIF text with Structure.from_str; 0 parse failures, 0 matching exceptions.
Compared all test structures against MP20 train+val entries with the same actual elemental fractional composition. Candidate grouping used parsed CIF contents, not the potentially incorrect test metadata formulas. Checked 853 test/reference candidate pairs after reuse across byte-identical test CIFs.
Decision: StructureMatcher.fit(test_structure, mp20_structure), symmetric=False. RMS diagnostics were computed only after fit passed; a non-null get_rms_dist alone was not used as the match decision.
Standard parameters: ltol=0.2, stol=0.3, angle_tol=5 degrees, primitive_cell=True, scale=True, attempt_supercell=False, allow_subset=False, SpeciesComparator.
Tighter parameters: ltol=0.01, stol=0.02, angle_tol=0.5 degrees, primitive_cell=True, scale=False, attempt_supercell=False, allow_subset=False, SpeciesComparator.
RMS and maximum-displacement values in the CSV are normalized by the free length per atom, not distances in angstroms.
This checks composition-preserving structural overlap with these local MP20 train/val files. It does not certify novelty against other model training corpora, anonymized prototypes, or every possible supercell/disorder matching policy.
